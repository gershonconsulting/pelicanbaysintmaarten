Arrival

Getting Here

FIRST THINGS FIRST — GO TO THE BEACH: Leave the unpacking for later. Keep a swimsuit at the top of your suitcase, grab a beach towel, and head straight to Buccaneer Beach down the street. You deserve your first piña colada by the sea—the suitcases can wait.

Finding Peridot Street

When you arrive on Billy Folly Road, look for The Greenhouse Restaurant. Peridot Street is directly opposite the restaurant. It is the very steep road going uphill—it may look a little intimidating, but it is the correct street to the house.
Peridot Street landmark: Once you see the sign on the right side of Greenhouse Restaurant, you are in front of Peridot Street.

Video IN, Video OUT

For safer check-in and checkout, record a short video of the house when you arrive and another just before you leave. This gives everyone a clear record of the property’s condition and helps avoid surprises if an issue is reported later. Please send both videos to Olivier via WhatsApp as soon as you record them.

Inside the home

Comfort, Balcony & Amenities

IMPORTANT — AIR CONDITIONING: DO NOT leave the air conditioning running all day or while you are away. Use it only when you are present in the house, and switch it off whenever you leave. Bedrooms normally cool in 10–20 minutes; the living areas take around 30 minutes.

Sand & Cleanliness

The house is only five minutes from the beach—an amazing location, but some beach sand inevitably finds its way inside despite thorough and repeated cleaning. Please keep the Main Entrance closed to minimize the amount of sand entering the house. To help keep the home clean, please leave shoes outside.

This is our family home. Our children and we live here part time, so please respect and care for it as if it were your own.

Main Entrance

The Main Entrance connects the main living area and terrace. Lock it whenever you leave the apartment.

Balcony Access

Balcony Access opens directly from the living room toward the terrace and sea view.
