Arrival

Getting Here

FIRST THINGS FIRST — GO TO THE BEACH: Leave the unpacking for later. Keep a swimsuit at the top of your suitcase, grab a beach towel, and head straight to Buccaneer Beach down the street. You deserve your first piña colada by the sea—the suitcases can wait.

Finding Peridot Street

When you arrive on Billy Folly Road, look for The Greenhouse Restaurant. Peridot Street is directly opposite the restaurant. It is the very steep road going uphill—it may look a little intimidating, but it is the correct street to the house.
Peridot Street landmark: Once you see the sign on the right side of Greenhouse Restaurant, you are in front of Peridot Street.

Video IN, Video OUT

For safer check-in and checkout, record a short video of the house when you arrive and another just before you leave. This gives everyone a clear record of the property’s condition and helps avoid surprises if an issue is reported later.

Inside the home

Comfort, Balcony & Amenities

IMPORTANT — AIR CONDITIONING: DO NOT leave the air conditioning running all day or while you are away. Use it only when you are present in the house, and switch it off whenever you leave. Bedrooms normally cool in 10–20 minutes; the living areas take around 30 minutes.
ELECTRICITY — 110V, JUST LIKE THE US: We are on the Dutch side of the island, so the whole apartment runs on 110 volts / 60 Hz with US-style flat-pin sockets (Type A/B). American and Canadian devices plug straight in. Guests arriving from Europe (including the French side) need a plug adapter, and any appliance not marked 100–240V also needs a voltage converter — a 220V hair dryer or curling iron will simply not work here.

Balcony Entrance

The balcony entrance connects the main living area and terrace. Lock it whenever you leave the apartment.

Living Room & View

The living room opens directly toward the terrace and the sea view.

Extra Mattress

An extra air mattress is stored in the kitchen. It may be used for additional sleeping space or for stargazing on the terrace. Please return it clean and dry after use.

2. Load and run the dishwasher.
3. Tie all garbage bags securely and place them outside in the designated location shown below.
4. Turn off lights, fans, and air conditioning.
5. Close and lock all doors and windows.
6. Reconnect the living-room camera.
7. Double-check for chargers, clothing, and toiletries.
8. Leave both house keys in the kitchen: the Gate key and the Rooms Door key.
VIDEO OUT: Before leaving, record a short final video of the house. Together with your arrival video, it provides a clear record of the property’s condition at check-in and checkout.
USED TOWELS & LINENS: Put them in the bathtub. There is no need to wash anything—we will take care of it.

Garbage — Before You Leave

Please do not leave any garbage inside the house when you check out. The cleaning lady will arrive shortly after your departure, but because the air conditioning will be turned off, any garbage left indoors can quickly create an unpleasant smell for the next guests.
