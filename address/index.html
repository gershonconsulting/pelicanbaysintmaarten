Step by step

The last few hundred metres.

One

Find The Greenhouse Restaurant

Drive along Billy Folly Road until you see the large green Greenhouse Restaurant sign. Peridot Street is directly opposite it.

Two

Take the steep road uphill

Turn onto the very steep road going up the hill. It looks a little intimidating — it is the right street. The house is at the top.

Three

Park in spot #1

Designated parking is to the right of the entrance. Spot #1 gives the easiest access to the door.

Four

Go through the gate to Entrance B

Enter through the exterior gate and head upstairs toward the terrace. Entrance B is the main entrance; Entrance A stays closed.

From SXM AirportAbout 15 minutes by car, depending on traffic and bridge openings.
On foot from the beachBuccaneer Beach is a short walk down the hill — the way back up is a real climb.
